Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

DSK Agency, Viruthachalam ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the enquiry form on our website, your details are sent to our WhatsApp number so we can reply. We do not store them on any server through this website.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service provider (such as Jio or the DTH company) when it is required to activate your connection, or when the law requires it.

Documents you give us are used only for your connection or service request. We take reasonable care to keep them safe and do not use them for any other purpose.

Our website links to other sites such as Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps and OTT apps. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us.
